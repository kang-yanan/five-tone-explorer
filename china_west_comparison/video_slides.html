怎么做？

How does it work?

① 填基本信息（年龄、音乐经验）
Enter demographics (age, music training)

② 闭眼聆听6段（5段五音+1段静息，随机顺序）
Listen to 6 segments (5 tones + 1 silent baseline, randomized)

③ 每段后标注：唤醒度（1-5）+ 情绪种类
Rate arousal (1-5) & emotion category after each

④ 看到个人匹配结果
View personal match results vs. traditional theory

⑤ 传感器后台记录聆听姿态
Accelerometer logs listening posture in background

⏱ 约5分钟  ·  匿名  ·  数据自动上传  |  ~5 min  ·  Anonymous  ·  Auto-save
